Winged Light Guide

Learn how Winged Light works and explore the realms of Sky: Children of the Light.

What is Winged Light?

Winged Light increases your cape's flight energy. Find glowing Children of Light throughout the world and collect them to strengthen your cape.

If you lose a Winged Light, it may return to a location you previously visited.
